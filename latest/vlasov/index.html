Vlasov

The continuum Vlasov equation on a two-dimensional phase space $\Omega = \Omega_x \times \Omega_v$ is a Lie-Poisson Hamiltonian system with respect to the canonical phase-space bracket $\{a, b\}_{(x, v)} = \partial_x a \, \partial_v b - \partial_v a \, \partial_x b$:

\[\{F, G\}_{\mathrm{LP}}[f] = \int_\Omega f(x, v) \, \left\{ \frac{\delta F}{\delta f}, \frac{\delta G}{\delta f} \right\}_{(x, v)} dx \, dv .\]

Why direct Eulerian projections fail the Jacobi identity

Projecting $f_h = \sum_{m = 1}^N c_m \Phi_m$ directly onto a finite-dimensional spline, spectral or finite-difference basis produces a state-linear bracket $\mathbb{J}_{ij}(c) = \sum_m c_{ij}^m c_m$ with tensor vlasov_galerkin_tensor. By the structure-constant theorem, $\mathbb{J}(c)$ satisfies the Jacobi identity if and only if $c_{ij}^m$ are the structure constants of a finite-dimensional Lie algebra. Polynomial splines, truncated Fourier series and the nine-point Arakawa stencil do not close under $\{\cdot, \cdot\}_{(x, v)}$, so their structure_constant_residual and jacobi_residual remain $O(1)$ under mesh refinement. Nor can a pointwise change of variables $f = \Phi(\bar{f})$ flatten the bracket in two phase-space dimensions, because $\{h A, h B\}_{(x, v)} \neq h^2 \{A, B\}_{(x, v)}$.

Four non-particle finite-dimensional Poisson brackets

This module provides four distinct non-particle routes that satisfy the Jacobi identity exactly at every finite dimension:

  1. Time-reversal parity splitting (velocity_parity_projectors, parity_vlasov_blocks, parity_vlasov_bracket, dlra_parity_vlasov_bracket): under velocity reflection $\mathcal{T}_v(x, v) = (x, L_v - v)$, the canonical bracket $\{\cdot, \cdot\}_{(x, v)}$ has odd parity. At any velocity-even equilibrium $f_0(x, L_v - v) = f_0(x, v)$, the $v$-even subspace (carrying charge density $\rho$) and $v$-odd subspace (carrying current density $j$) are Lagrangian subspaces of the frozen Lie-Poisson operator $\mathbb{J}(f_0)$ (J_pp == 0, J_mm == 0), producing a constant off-diagonal Poisson matrix $\mathbb{J}_{\mathrm{par}}$ on spline, grid and $O(r) \times O(r)$ dynamic-low-rank Stiefel frames.
  2. Canonical Clebsch potentials (canonical_vlasov_bracket, clebsch_vlasov_tensor, clebsch_vlasov_map, clebsch_pushforward_matrix): representing the distribution through canonical potentials $f_h = f_{0, h} + \Pi_h\{\alpha_h, \beta_h\}_{(x, v)}$ lifts the dynamics to $(Q, P) \in (\mathbb{R}^{2N}, \mathbb{J}_{2N})$, where the bracket is constant and canonical while total mass $\int_\Omega f_h \, dx \, dv$ is conserved identically by the B-spline partition of unity. The discrete Clebsch non-submersion theorem shows why the state must remain $(Q, P)$: the pushforward bivector $\Pi_{mn}(Q, P) = -Q^T [B_m, B_n] P$ descends to a Lie-Poisson bracket on $c$ if and only if the slices $B[m, :, :]$ close under the matrix commutator.
  3. Finite-mode von Neumann-Poisson quantisation on $\mathfrak{u}(N)^*$ and spectral pushforwards (un_hermitian_basis, un_structure_constants, weyl_vlasov_matrix, spectral_pushforward_bracket): quantising the configuration torus into $N = 2K + 1$ modes via compressed Weyl quantisation yields an exact Lie-Poisson bracket on $\mathfrak{u}(N)^*$ that is exact on the inner mode block for symbols of degree $\le 1$ in $v$ and approximates Vlasov to $O(\hbar^2)$ with leading Wigner dispersion. Pushing forward along $V = \tfrac{1}{2}e^{2W} \succ 0$ yields the positive-definite Bogoliubov-Kubo-Mori logarithmic-mean Poisson bracket.
  4. Pointwise-flat velocity-moment / waterbag brackets (vlasov_moment_flat_bracket, vlasov_moment_direct_bracket): restricting to observables of degree $\le 1$ in $v$ closes into the semidirect product $\mathrm{Vect}(\Omega_x) \ltimes C^\infty(\Omega_x)$ on $(\rho, m)$. In one spatial dimension the pointwise chart $(\rho_i, u_i = m_i / \rho_i)$ flattens the bracket into a constant skew-symmetric matrix for every discrete derivative $\mathbb{K}_a$ (periodic or wall-bounded), so its pushforward $\mathbb{J}(\rho, m) = D\Phi_N \mathbb{K}_2 D\Phi_N^T$ satisfies the Jacobi identity identically, whereas direct collocation vlasov_moment_direct_bracket violates Jacobi at $O(1)$.

GeometricBrackets.clebsch_pushforward_matrix — Method
clebsch_pushforward_matrix(B::AbstractArray{<:Any, 3}, Q::AbstractVector, P::AbstractVector)

The $M \times M$ pushforward bivector

\[\Pi_{mn}(Q, P) = \{c_m, c_n\}_{\mathrm{can}}(Q, P) = (\nabla_Q c_m)^T (\nabla_P c_n) - (\nabla_P c_m)^T (\nabla_Q c_n)\]

induced on the Eulerian coefficient space $\mathbb{R}^M$ by the canonical bracket canonical_vlasov_bracket under clebsch_vlasov_map.

By the discrete Clebsch non-submersion theorem, when each slice $B_m = B[m, :, :]$ is skew-symmetric, $\Pi_{mn}(Q, P) = -Q^T [B_m, B_n] P$, so $\Pi(Q, P)$ descends to a linear Lie-Poisson bracket on $c$ if and only if the slices $B[m, :, :]$ close under the matrix commutator.

GeometricBrackets.clebsch_vlasov_tensor — Method
clebsch_vlasov_tensor(space::TensorSplineSpace{T, 2}) where {T}

The rank-3 discrete Clebsch bilinear coupling tensor $B[m, i, j] = \mathcal{B}_{m, ij}$ for $f_h = f_{0, h} + \Pi_h\{\alpha_h, \beta_h\}_{(x, v)}$ on space:

\[\mathcal{B}_{m, ij} = \sum_{p = 1}^N (\mathbb{M}^{-1})_{mp} \int_\Omega \Phi_p(x, v) \, \{\Phi_i, \Phi_j\}_{(x, v)} \, dx \, dv .\]

For each mode m, the slice B[m, :, :] is skew-symmetric, and because $\sum_{p = 1}^N \Phi_p(x, v) \equiv 1$ on a B-spline partition of unity, the basis integrals $b_m = \int_\Omega \Phi_m \, dx \, dv$ annihilate the Clebsch perturbation identically on a periodic domain: $\sum_m b_m \mathcal{B}_{m, ij} = 0$.

GeometricBrackets.parity_vlasov_blocks — Method
parity_vlasov_blocks(space::TensorSplineSpace{T, 2}, f0; orthonormal::Bool = false) where {T}

The four parity blocks (J_pp, J_pm, J_mp, J_mm) of the linearised Vlasov Lie-Poisson operator at background f0 in the $(v\text{-even}, v\text{-odd})$ decomposition from velocity_parity_projectors.

For any velocity-even background $f_0(x, L_v - v) = f_0(x, v)$ on a velocity basis with reflection symmetry (such as Free() or Dirichlet() on the velocity axis), the even and odd subspaces are Lagrangian (maximally isotropic): J_pp and J_mm vanish to machine precision, and J_mp == -J_pm'.

GeometricBrackets.parity_vlasov_bracket — Method
parity_vlasov_bracket(A::AbstractMatrix{T}) where {T}
parity_vlasov_bracket(space::TensorSplineSpace{T, 2}, f0; orthonormal::Bool = false) where {T}

The constant skew-symmetric block-off-diagonal Poisson bracket

\[\mathbb{J}_{\mathrm{par}} = \begin{pmatrix} \mathbb{O}_{N_+ \times N_+} & \mathbb{A} \\ -\mathbb{A}^T & \mathbb{O}_{N_- \times N_-} \end{pmatrix}\]

on the time-reversal parity-split phase space $(Q^{(0)}, P^{(0)}) \in \mathbb{R}^{N_+ + N_-}$, wrapped as a ConstantBracket. Because $\mathbb{J}_{\mathrm{par}}$ is independent of $(Q^{(0)}, P^{(0)})$, its derivative tensor vanishes identically and it satisfies the Jacobi identity at every resolution and for every coupling matrix $\mathbb{A}$.

GeometricBrackets.spectral_pushforward_bracket — Method
spectral_pushforward_bracket(φ, dφ, w::AbstractVector{T}; ħ::Real = one(T)) where {T}
spectral_pushforward_bracket(φ, dφ, d2φ, w::AbstractVector{T}; ħ::Real = one(T)) where {T}

The $N^2 \times N^2$ Poisson matrix J and derivative tensor dJ (with $dJ[m, a, b] = \partial J_{ab} / \partial v_m$) in $v$-coordinates of the pushforward of the $\mathfrak{u}(N)^*$ Lie-Poisson bracket along the monotone spectral diffeomorphism $V = \varphi(W)$, evaluated analytically at $W = \sum_{a = 1}^{N^2} w_a E_a$ in the basis un_hermitian_basis via first- and second-order Daleckii-Krein divided differences $\mathcal{L}_\varphi(\lambda_j, \lambda_k)$ and $\mathcal{L}_\varphi^{[2]}(\lambda_i, \lambda_j, \lambda_\ell)$.

For $\varphi(\lambda) = \tfrac{1}{2}e^{2\lambda}$ (corresponding to the quadratic structure function $c(w) = w^2$), $V \succ 0$ is strictly positive-definite and the Fréchet derivative $D\varphi(W)$ acts on eigenbasis entries by twice the logarithmic mean, $2(v_j - v_k)/(\log v_j - \log v_k)$, yielding the Bogoliubov-Kubo-Mori logarithmic-mean Poisson bracket on $\mathfrak{u}(N)^*$ which satisfies the Jacobi identity to machine precision in both Float64 and BigFloat.

The method without d2φ takes $\varphi''$ from a complex step on dφ, so there dφ must accept a complex argument.

un_hermitian_basis(N::Integer, ::Type{T} = Float64) where {T}

An orthonormal basis $\{E_a\}_{a = 1}^{N^2}$ of $N \times N$ Hermitian matrices with respect to the trace inner product $\operatorname{tr}(E_a E_b) = \delta_{ab}$: the $N$ diagonal projectors $e_j e_j^T$, the $N(N - 1)/2$ real symmetric off-diagonal matrices $(e_j e_k^T + e_k e_j^T)/\sqrt{2}$, and the $N(N - 1)/2$ imaginary skew-symmetric matrices $i(e_j e_k^T - e_k e_j^T)/\sqrt{2}$.

GeometricBrackets.un_structure_constants — Method
un_structure_constants(N::Integer, ::Type{T} = Float64; ħ::Real = one(T)) where {T}

The real $N^2 \times N^2 \times N^2$ structure-constant tensor

\[C[m, a, b] = c_{ab}^m = \operatorname{tr}\!\left(E_m \, \frac{1}{i\hbar}[E_a, E_b]\right)\]

of the finite-mode von Neumann-Poisson bracket on $\mathfrak{u}(N)^*$ in the trace-orthonormal Hermitian basis un_hermitian_basis. Because $\mathfrak{u}(N)$ is a compact Lie algebra and $\operatorname{tr}(E_a E_b) = \delta_{ab}$ is ad-invariant, $c_{ab}^m$ is completely antisymmetric in $(m, a, b)$ and satisfies structure_constant_residual to machine precision at every $N$.

GeometricBrackets.velocity_parity_projectors — Method
velocity_parity_projectors(space::TensorSplineSpace{T, 2}; orthonormal::Bool = false) where {T}

The embedding matrices (U_plus, U_minus) of the $v$-even and $v$-odd subspaces of a two-dimensional tensor-product spline space under velocity reflection $\mathcal{T}_v(x, v) = (x, L_v - v)$, which reverses the velocity B-spline index $j \mapsto n_v + 1 - j$.

When orthonormal = false, the columns of U_plus and U_minus are Euclidean-orthonormal ($U_\pm^T U_\pm = \mathbb{I}$, $U_+^T U_- = \mathbb{O}$). When orthonormal = true, they are $\mathbb{M}$-orthonormalised via the symmetric Cholesky factorisation of the mass matrix ($U_\pm^T \mathbb{M} U_\pm = \mathbb{I}$, $U_+^T \mathbb{M} U_- = \mathbb{O}$), forming the parity-adapted B-spline Stiefel frame.

GeometricBrackets.vlasov_galerkin_tensor — Method
vlasov_galerkin_tensor(space::TensorSplineSpace{T, 2}) where {T}

The rank-3 tensor $C[m, i, j] = c_{ij}^m$ of the direct Galerkin projection of the two-dimensional Vlasov Lie-Poisson bracket

\[\{F, G\}_{\mathrm{LP}}[f] = \int_\Omega f(x, v) \, \left( \frac{\partial}{\partial x}\frac{\delta F}{\delta f} \, \frac{\partial}{\partial v}\frac{\delta G}{\delta f} - \frac{\partial}{\partial v}\frac{\delta F}{\delta f} \, \frac{\partial}{\partial x}\frac{\delta G}{\delta f} \right) dx \, dv\]

onto space, with entries

\[c_{ij}^m = \sum_{p, q = 1}^N (\mathbb{M}^{-1})_{ip} \, (\mathbb{M}^{-1})_{jq} \int_\Omega \Phi_m(x, v) \, \{\Phi_p, \Phi_q\}_{(x, v)} \, dx \, dv .\]

The resulting linear Lie-Poisson matrix lie_poisson_matrix(C, c) is antisymmetric for every coefficient vector c, but its structure_constant_residual and jacobi_residual are $O(1)$ because polynomial B-splines do not close under the canonical phase-space Poisson bracket $\{\cdot, \cdot\}_{(x, v)}$.

GeometricBrackets.vlasov_moment_direct_bracket — Method
vlasov_moment_direct_bracket(K::AbstractMatrix, ρ::AbstractVector, m::AbstractVector)

The $2N \times 2N$ matrix J and derivative tensor dJ ($dJ[k, i, j] = \partial J_{ij} / \partial z_k$) of the naive direct-collocation discretisation of the semidirect-product Vlasov moment bracket:

\[\mathbb{J}_{\mathrm{dir}}(\rho, m) = \begin{pmatrix} \mathbb{O}_N & \mathbb{K}\mathrm{diag}(\rho) \\ -\mathrm{diag}(\rho)\mathbb{K}^T & \mathrm{diag}(m)\mathbb{K} - \mathbb{K}^T\mathrm{diag}(m) \end{pmatrix} .\]

Unlike vlasov_moment_flat_bracket, this linear-in-state discretisation violates the Jacobi identity at $O(1)$ because the $(\rho, m)$-block and $(m, m)$-block fail to satisfy the Leibniz product rule on a discrete stencil.

GeometricBrackets.vlasov_moment_flat_bracket — Method
vlasov_moment_flat_bracket(Ka::AbstractMatrix, ρ::AbstractVector, m::AbstractVector;
                           Kb::AbstractMatrix = zeros(promote_type(eltype(Ka), eltype(ρ), eltype(m)), size(Ka)))

The $2N \times 2N$ discrete Poisson matrix J and derivative tensor dJ ($dJ[k, i, j] = \partial J_{ij} / \partial z_k$) of the pointwise-flat semidirect-product Vlasov moment / waterbag bracket on $(\rho, m)$ (density $\rho_i = \int f \, dv$ and momentum density $m_i = \int v f \, dv$), obtained by pushing forward the constant bracket

\[\mathbb{K}_2 = \begin{pmatrix} \mathbb{O}_N & \mathbb{K}_a \\ -\mathbb{K}_a^T & \mathbb{K}_b \end{pmatrix}\]

from the flat velocity chart $(\rho_i, u_i = m_i / \rho_i)$ via $D\Phi_N = \begin{psmallmatrix} \mathbb{I} & \mathbb{O} \\ \mathrm{diag}(u) & \mathrm{diag}(\rho) \end{psmallmatrix}$:

\[\mathbb{J}(\rho, m) = D\Phi_N \, \mathbb{K}_2 \, D\Phi_N^T = \begin{pmatrix} \mathbb{O}_N & \mathbb{K}_a \mathrm{diag}(\rho) \\ -\mathrm{diag}(\rho) \mathbb{K}_a^T & \mathrm{diag}(u) \mathbb{K}_a \mathrm{diag}(\rho) - \mathrm{diag}(\rho) \mathbb{K}_a^T \mathrm{diag}(u) + \mathrm{diag}(\rho) \mathbb{K}_b \mathrm{diag}(\rho) \end{pmatrix} .\]

Satisfies the Jacobi identity identically for every matrix $\mathbb{K}_a$ and every antisymmetric matrix $\mathbb{K}_b = -\mathbb{K}_b^T$ on both periodic and bounded domains. Kb enters through its antisymmetric part $\tfrac{1}{2}(\mathbb{K}_b - \mathbb{K}_b^T)$, so J is antisymmetric for any Kb.
